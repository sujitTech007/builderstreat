MISSION STATEMENT

Builderstreat Mission

At Builderstreat, our mission is to revolutionize construction and demolition waste recycling by delivering seamless and sustainable solutions for material exchange. We are committed to building a circular economy where overabundant materials are repurposed, waste is minimized and environmental impact is significantly reduced.

By leveraging our innovative platform, we empower businesses and individuals to collaborate and contribute to a greener future.

We prioritize transparency, integrity and efficiency, coupled with our commitment to being the trusted partner in the construction industry as we accelerate the shift toward a regenerative world.

Frequently Asked Questions

Explore our FAQs for quick answers to common inquiries and concerns.

Builderstreat is a technology company specializing in creating solutions that expand the capabilities of the Construction and Demolition (C&D) waste recycling industry. Our digital marketplace connects companies and individuals with C&D waste to other parties that will either use it for a new purpose or efficiently recycle or dispose of it, promoting a more sustainable construction industry.

Builderstreat facilitates the exchange of various materials generated from construction and demolition activities, including but not limited to concrete, wood, metal, plastic, glass, and asphalt. We aim to promote the reuse and recycling of these materials to minimize waste and environmental impact.

Now, Builderstreat operates primarily in Canada and the United States. However, we have plans to expand into other regions in the future as part of our growth strategy.

Yes, Builderstreat provides customer support to assist users with any questions, issues, or concerns they may have. Users can reach out to our support team through the platform's messaging system or contact us via email or phone for assistance.

Builderstreat is designed for a wide range of users within the construction industry, including large construction companies, individual contractors, waste management businesses, and suppliers of recycled materials. Whether you have surplus materials to sell or are looking for specific materials for your project, Builderstreat provides a platform for connecting with relevant parties

Builderstreat facilitates transactions between buyers and sellers on the platform. Payments are securely processed through the platform, and transaction funds are held in escrow until both parties confirm completion. This ensures transparency and protects the interests of all parties involved in the transaction.

To get started with Builderstreat, simply visit our website, and sign up for a membership. Once registered, you can create a profile, list materials, browse listings, and connect with other users on the platform to start exchanging C&D waste materials efficiently and sustainably.
